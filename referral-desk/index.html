Why we built it

Built by someone who knows the referral process firsthand

After managing high-volume referrals, diagnostic imaging orders, and prior authorizations for healthcare organizations, one thing became clear: referral coordinators spend far too much time searching for information that should be easy to find.

From locating the correct department fax number to navigating payer requirements and facility-specific documentation, these small obstacles add up.

We created The Referral Desk to bring those essential resources together in one organized, accessible workspace, so coordinators can spend less time searching and more time getting patients scheduled.

FAQ

Common questions

What happens to patient information?

The Smart Packet Builder works with the order and insurance card you add, but those files are read on your own computer, in your web browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on The Referral Desk's servers. Your account only records how many packets you've made. The other tools, like PA resources, the facility directory and criteria checks, don't need patient information at all.

Can I use the Packet Builder now?

It's being tested with fictional patients right now. Waitlist members will be the first to try it.

How do you keep it accurate?

Every entry shows the date it was last verified, and members can report a change with one click. Requirements can differ by member plan, so always confirm in the payer portal before you submit.

Which areas do you cover?

We're launching with the Mid-Ohio Valley and West Virginia payers, then expanding by region based on waitlist demand. Tell us your area when you sign up.

Does it replace Availity?

No. Use it alongside your payer portals. It shows the latest PA reference and where to confirm it, and the portal is where you confirm and submit.
